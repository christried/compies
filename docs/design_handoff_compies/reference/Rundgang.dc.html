Rundgang

Eight steps, one thing at a time: the page goes dark except the thing being explained, a bubble says one or two sentences, and Compibert's face sits on the bubble's corner. The bubble is the app's popover (3 px ink border, 18 px corners, modal shadow), the dark is the sheet scrim (--cp-overlay), the ring is Sonne. Two options for Compibert, A still and B gently moving; everything else is shared. No new tokens.

The same on a phone

On the phone the four slot cards stack, so the poll is taller than the window. The step spots the first card only, because that is what the sentence is about (Ja, Meh, Nein). Rule: each step names the smallest thing its sentence explains. Bubble full width minus 12 px, face 56 px.

Hallo · Zeigt · Freut sich

All three come from existing rig states on the head crop: „Hallo“ = wave (grin, brow up, head tilted −6°), „Zeigt“ = idle with the pupils set toward the thing, „Freut sich“ = cheer (happy eyes, grin). Row A holds them; row B is the live rig (blink, eyes, nod, two hops at the end). Zeigt is shown pointing up-right, the most common case (bubble under the thing).

Nothing glides
