Block 5, part 2: chat, activity feed, emotes, the date poll and push notifications. The chat, emotes and date poll all work. Emote art is labelled placeholders, with an illustration brief.

Chat

Everyone in the session can write: teams, bench, stands. The host can delete messages. Shown at the side panel's real width (340 px).

Use for. Talk around the game. Messages from one person within 2 minutes group under one name line. The name line says where they sit (team mark, „Bench“, „Tribüne“). Own messages sit right, in yellow.
Don't. No reactions, threads, DMs, images or links previews in 1.0. Deleted messages leave a dashed „Vom Host gelöscht“ in place so the conversation still makes sense. Game events never go in the chat; they go in Aktivität.
Accessibility. role="log" announces new messages politely, without stealing focus. Enter sends. The delete button is always in the tab order for the host, not only on hover. Failed sends keep the text and offer retry right under the message.
Desktop and mobile. Desktop: right side panel, 340 px, Chat and Aktivität as tabs. Mobile: two entries in the bottom tab bar with an unread count; the composer sticks above the keyboard.

Aktivität

Automatic events, grouped by game. Switch the tab in the panel above to see it in place. Event types:
{{ e.n }}{{ e.ex }}
Use for. Everything the app does on its own: joins, moves, draws, vetoes, bans, picks, results, corrections, date changes. Stored with the chat in the archive.
Don't. Never post a veto before the reveal. Don't log every bench ↔ stands hop during a game; collapse repeated moves within 1 minute into one line.
Accessibility. Icons are decorative; the sentence carries the meaning and names the team in words. Game headers are real headings, so screen readers can jump between games.
Desktop and mobile. Same list; the game header sticks while scrolling. The unread counter on the tab caps at 99+.

Emotes

Four emotes that rise above the sender's avatar on the board. Short cooldown against spam.
Live: you are Kitty
{{ p.label }}
{{ emoMsg }}
Illustration brief
  • Style: Compibert's hands, face and props in the sticker style: 3 px ink line, flat accent fills, white die-cut rim, hard offset shadow. No gradients, no system emoji.
  • Klatschen: two little claws clapping, three short motion ticks. Fill: yellow.
  • Freudentränen: Compibert's face laughing, two big tear arcs. Fill: mint.
  • Wütend: Compibert's face, steam puffs, furrowed brow. Fill: hot red.
  • Compibert-Brüller: full head, mouth wide open, jagged roar lines. Fill: pink. The only emote with its own longer sound (the roar).
  • Delivery: SVG, 96 × 96 artboard, readable at 32 px. A 3-frame bounce (squash, stretch, settle) per emote for the rise.
Don't. No custom or extra emotes in 1.0. Cooldown 2 s per person; the room plays at most 3 emote sounds per second, extras are silent but still show. Emotes never cover the veto button or the timer.
Accessibility. Emote buttons have names; the cooldown ring has a text equivalent. Floating emotes are decorative and not announced. Reduced motion: the emote pops in place above the avatar and fades, no rise.
Desktop and mobile. Desktop: an emote button in the dock opens this row as a popover. Mobile: a smile button next to the composer opens a bottom sheet with the four, 72 px each.

Everyone votes Ja, Wenn nötig or Nein per slot and sees everyone else's vote. The app marks the best slot; the host confirms it.

Use for. The „Terminumfrage“ phase, in the lobby's stage. Best slot: most Ja, then most Wenn nötig, then earliest. It moves live as people vote. Your own row is always on top.
Don't. No anonymous voting, no comments per slot. „Bester Termin“ is only a suggestion; nothing is fixed until the host confirms. Rescheduling keeps the teams.
Accessibility. A real table with row and column headers. Each vote has a shape (check, dash, cross, dashed ring) as well as a colour. Your three options per slot are a radio group named after the slot.
Desktop and mobile. Desktop: the table. Mobile: one card per slot with your three buttons full width and the others as an avatar row under „Ja“, „Wenn nötig“, „Nein“.

Push-Benachrichtigungen

Via the PWA, desktop too. Ask in the app first, at a moment where the benefit is obvious; only then trigger the browser prompt.
Sollen wir dir Bescheid geben?Wenn der Termin steht, eine Stunde vorher, und wenn es losgeht. Mehr nicht.
Ja, Benachrichtigungen an Später
Soft ask: shown after your first vote in a date poll. „Später“ asks again after the next session, never twice in one.
Use for. Only the seven events in the brief. Title says what happened, body says what to do next. Tapping opens the matching place in the app (poll, lobby, stage, ceremony).
Don't. No push while the person has the session open in a visible tab. No chat messages, no emotes, no marketing. „Spiel gezogen“ and „Ergebnis“ only go to people not currently in the lobby.
Accessibility. Copy works without the icon and without the session name being visible (it always names the session). Under 120 characters so lock screens don't cut the action.
