Was spielen wir?

Block 4: how the next game gets chosen. Game cards and the pool, the five selection modes, the Glücksrad, the 60-second veto window, the coin flip and Pick & Ban. The wheel, veto window, coin and Pick & Ban demos all work. Sound follows the mute switch in the rail.

Game card

One game from the library. The host's uploaded image fills the cover; without one, initials on an accent colour. Stamps show what happened to it this session.

Row: lists, side panel, mobile
Use for. Library, pool picker, Pick & Ban board, Loser's Pick. Card in grids, row in lists and on mobile. „Passt“ marks a suggestion for the session's format and player count.
Don't. One stamp at a time. Never hide a banned or vetoed game: dim it, strike it through, keep it in place so everyone can read the history. No ratings, genres or descriptions on the card.
Accessibility. The card is wrapped in a button when it's selectable, with the state in its name („Rocket League, gebannt von Nacht Eulen“). Dimmed states also change the outline to dashed and strike the name.
Desktop and mobile. Desktop: card grid with columns of at least 170 px. Mobile: row layout below 480 px, 60 px tall, with the stamp as a chip on the right.

Use for. Lucky Wheel, the first game in Loser's Pick, ties for last place, and the tiebreaker (its own wheel with the tiebreaker pool). Skin: Show-Rad, with bulbs that blink at rest, chase during the spin and flash on the result. Spin length is a host setting: 3 to 10 s, default 5 s, set in setup and changeable any time; the server sends it with the result so every screen stops together.
Don't. Never let the client decide the result or shorten the spin for one person. Slice colours are the four accents, never team colours. No sound loop; the ticks come from the actual rotation.
Accessibility. The wheel is an image with a count; the result is announced in a live region („Das Glücksrad sagt: Rocket League“). With reduced motion it spins once in 1.2 s with no ticks or pointer flick, then shows the result the same way.
Desktop and mobile. Desktop: up to 440 px in the stage. Mobile: full width minus 32 px. Above 12 slices the labels shrink to 12 px and truncate at 16 characters.

Veto window

60 seconds from the moment a game is set: 30 s clip, then 30 s to decide. Your team sees its own veto right away; everyone else's is revealed at 0:00, all at once. Demo as Kitty, team Wilde Gurken Bande.
Use for. Lucky Wheel, Loser's Pick, Loser's Advantage and the tiebreaker. Vetoes are pooled per team (one per member), max one per team per game. The button stays reachable while the clip plays (z-index veto 46 above clip 44).
Don't. Never leak other teams' vetoes before 0:00: no counters, no sounds, no activity entries. No undo. Never pause the timer for one person; only the host can skip the clip, and the window keeps running.
Accessibility. The timer is role="timer", silent, and announced at 30 s, 10 s and the reveal. The last 10 seconds turn red and the timer pulses on each second (still in reduced motion: colour only). Autoplay blocked: „Ton aktivieren“ in the clip, never a modal.
Desktop and mobile. Desktop: clip in the stage, veto bar under it. Mobile: the clip fills the width at the top, team tiles become a 2×2 grid, and the veto button pins to the bottom above the safe area.

Münzwurf

Decides who bans first in Pick & Ban. Kopf is Compibert's head, Schwanz his rear end.
Kopf: Wilde Gurken Bande Schwanz: Nacht Eulen
{{ coinSub }}
Use for. Only the start of Pick & Ban, before the first ban. The host throws; everyone sees the same flip at the same time. The sides are assigned before the throw and stay on screen. Take: Zeitlupe. The coin shoots up, nearly freezes at the apex while the camera orbits it once, then drops, lands flat, and the camera snaps to a top view of the result.
Don't. No re-throw button for players. Don't use the coin for anything else (ties for last use the wheel). The tail side needs its own illustration; the placeholder must not ship.
Accessibility. The result is announced as text („Kopf! Wilde Gurken Bande bannt zuerst“). Reduced motion: no toss or spin, the coin cross-fades to the result side.
Desktop and mobile. Centre stage on both: a 360 px stage with a 140 px coin on desktop, full width minus 32 px on mobile. The take runs 3.6 s (rise 0.6 s, orbit at the apex 1.6 s, drop and settle 0.8 s, camera to top view 0.6 s), then the result holds 1.5 s before the ban board takes over.

Pick & Ban

Bans first, then picks, in a snake. Two teams of two: two bans each, then three picks. Pick order is play order. No clock. In the demo you act for whichever team is up.

Use for. The order strip is the source of truth: B for ban, P for pick, team mark, done tick. Two parties: 1, then 2 each. Three or more snake back and forth (A, B, C, C, B, A). The team that didn't start banning starts picking.
Don't. No timer, no auto-pick. Don't show the hold button to teams that aren't up; they see whose turn it is. Banned games stay on the board, dimmed, so the reasoning stays visible.
Accessibility. Two steps (select, then hold) so a misclick can't ban. The turn banner is a polite live region („Nacht Eulen bannt, 2 von 2“). The hold works with Space/Enter like the veto.
Desktop and mobile. Desktop: strip on top, card grid, hold bar under it. Mobile: the strip scrolls sideways with the current step centred, cards switch to rows, and the hold bar pins to the bottom.

Turn banners

One sentence at the top of the stage that says who decides right now. Used by Loser's Pick, Loser's Advantage and host overrides.
{{ b.k }}
{{ b.t }} Nur du siehst das
Use for. Any moment where one party or the host holds the decision. The team mark leads when a team decides; an icon leads otherwise.
Don't. One banner at a time, one sentence, no buttons inside (actions live in the dock or the stage). Never blame („ihr seid Letzte“); say what happens.
Accessibility. Polite live region; the change of turn is announced once. Never only the team colour: the name is always in the sentence.
Desktop and mobile. Desktop: top of the stage, under the session header. Mobile: same place, 18 px text, the mark shrinks to 28 px.
