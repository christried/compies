Use for. Every in-session screen. The header always shows the session name and phase; the stage shows what's happening right now (board, wheel, clip, result); the side panel holds the conversation and the standings.
Don't. No second navigation, no left sidebar. The side panel never collapses during the veto window. Nothing scrolls the header away.
Accessibility. Landmarks: header, main (stage), aside (panel), the dock is a labelled toolbar. A skip link jumps to the stage. Panel tabs follow the tabs pattern from Basics.
Layout. Panel tabs: Chat, Aktivität, Tabelle, Leute (four max). Stage max width 1100 px, centred; above 1440 px the whole shell stops growing and centres on the paper.

Host dock

Floating at the bottom centre of the stage, wherever the host sits (team, bench, stands). Its buttons follow the phase; one primary at a time.

Use for. The host's actions for the current phase, max three buttons plus „Mehr“ (Link erneuern, Anzahl Spiele, Drehdauer, Nächstes Spiel setzen, Session beenden). „Nächstes Spiel setzen“ is in „Mehr“ in every phase.
Don't. Never cover the veto bar: during the veto window the dock moves up above it (z 30 vs 46). Disabled dock buttons keep their reason in a tooltip and in the button's description.
Accessibility. role="toolbar" with arrow-key navigation. Shortcut: H focuses the dock from anywhere in the session (shown in the „Mehr“ menu).
Desktop and mobile. Desktop: floating pill, 20 px above the stage bottom. Mobile: a 56 px crown button bottom right, above the tab bar, which opens the same actions as a bottom sheet.

Use for. Tabs: Spielbrett, Tabelle, Chat, Aktivität, Leute. „Spielbrett“ turns into the stage when something happens and shows a pulsing dot if you're elsewhere. Unread counts on Chat and Aktivität.
Don't. No hamburger menu in a session. Profile, archive and settings live behind your avatar in the header. Never hide the tab bar during the veto window; the veto button sits above it.
Accessibility. The tab bar is a nav with aria-current="page". Badges are part of the label („Chat, 3 ungelesen“). Labels never hide; icons alone aren't enough.
Layout. Tab bar 60 px plus the safe area. Host button 56 px, 16 px above the tab bar. Content padding 16 px. The keyboard pushes the composer up; the tab bar hides only while typing.

Connection and loading states

What the whole app does when it's starting, losing the connection, or can't open a session.

Use for. Banners for live-session problems (they push content down, never cover it). Full-screen states only when there's nothing to show: first load, invalid link, session gone, crash.
Don't. Never blame the user. Never lose input: chat drafts, words and votes are queued while offline and sent on reconnect. Reconnects shorter than 2 s show nothing.
Accessibility. Reconnecting is role="status"; lost connection is role="alert". Full-screen states move focus to their heading.
Server-timed moments. If you rejoin mid-spin or mid-veto, you land in the current second of it, not at its start. Missed reveals replay in 1 s from the activity feed.
