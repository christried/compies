Combinations that don't work
Pixel × Hand-drawn. Two lo-fi textures with opposite logic: snapped to a grid vs. deliberately wobbly. Both cost readability, and together they look unfinished rather than intentional.
Memphis × Hand-drawn. Double decoration noise. The 8 team colours drown in decor colours, and there's no calm surface left for chat or the veto countdown.
Memphis × Picnic big type. Everything shouts at once. Live moments have no quiet stage to stand out from, so the wheel feels like every other screen.
Ko-fi × Pixel. Soft and cozy against hard arcade: it reads as two products glued together. On cream, pixel art also loses its "live moment" punch.
Swiss × Ko-fi. Technically clean, but they cancel each other out. Rounded Swiss turns into generic SaaS, and no character is left for Compibert to live in.
Pixel as the entire base. Body text, chat and settings in pixel fonts tire the eyes over a 3-hour evening, and German compounds overflow. Pixel works as a layer, not as a foundation.
