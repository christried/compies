Use for. Every person, everywhere: board, chat, activity, poll results, leaderboard, ceremony. Rim slots are fixed: team mark bottom-left, host crown top-right, presence dot bottom-right.
Don't. Don't tint the avatar itself with the team colour (the tints are neutral on purpose). Don't add more rim badges; mood and emotes float above the avatar, never on it. No square avatars.

Use for. Pattern only on surfaces with no text on top: zone header strips, wheel slices, leaderboard bars. Chip or solid colour where text sits.
Don't. Never put text directly on a pattern. Never use team colours for UI states (success, error) or for badges. Never show a colour without its symbol.

Use for. The lobby, from the date poll until the ceremony. A drag lifts the avatar (scale, tilt, hard shadow); valid zones light up in the focus colour, invalid ones go dashed red and the reason rides along under the avatar.
Don't. Never move someone optimistically into the last slot: wait for the server, then bounce the loser back with a spring and say who was faster. Never hide the reason for a locked zone. No drag handles; the avatar is the handle.
Accessibility. Dragging always has a click alternative: Enter or a click on an avatar opens „verschieben nach“, focus moves into it, Esc returns to the avatar. Every result is announced („Kitty ist jetzt auf der Bench“). Zones are labelled groups with their count („Wilde Gurken Bande, 3 von 3“).
Desktop and mobile. Desktop: drag and drop, teams in a grid, bench and stands below. Mobile: teams stack as rows, bench and stands collapse to counters, and tapping an avatar opens the „Wohin?“ bottom sheet. Long-press drag (400 ms) works too, but the sheet is the main way.

Use for. Count pill top-right is always „filled / size“. A lock in the pill means no one gets in right now; the line under the zone says why and until when.
Don't. Don't grey out a locked zone: people still need to read who is in it. Don't show empty slots as „+“ when nobody can join; show the lock.
Accessibility. Drop target and refusal differ in line style (solid focus vs. dashed error), not only in colour. The reason is real text, also given in the move panel.
Desktop and mobile. Same states. On mobile the lock reason sits in the sheet next to the disabled option instead of under the zone.

Player list

The same people as rows: for the side panel, screen readers, and the host's quick moves. Live with the board above.

Use for. Side panel tab „Leute“, the host's substitution flow, and as the accessible twin of the board. Sorted by teams in order, then bench, then stands; online before offline within a zone.
Don't. Don't offer actions the person can't take: players only get a menu on their own row. No kick or ban in 1.0; the host's only tools are moves.
Accessibility. A list of rows with a menu button (aria-expanded). Menu items are reachable but marked aria-disabled with their reason in the item. Esc closes the menu and returns focus.
Desktop and mobile. Desktop: a popover menu, 340 px panel width. Mobile: a full-width list in the „Leute“ tab, and the menu opens the same „Wohin?“ sheet as the board.
