Typography

Lilita One shouts, Bricolage Grotesque talks, Bungee keeps score.
Headlines and timers, --cp-font-display Lilita One Titles, section and card headings, team names, and the big countdowns. Chunky and slightly condensed, so long German compounds still fit a zone header. It has one weight, so hierarchy comes from size and colour, never from bold. Google Fonts, OFL, weight 400 only. Never synthesize bold.
Scores, --cp-font-num 0123456789 Bungee for points, ranks, scores and the podium. Timers use Lilita One instead, so a countdown reads as a headline, not a score. Both go through CpNum: every digit sits in a fixed slot (Bungee 0.74 em, Lilita 0.6 em), so nothing wobbles. {{ digitTxt }}
Text and UI, --cp-font-sans Bricolage Grotesque Body, buttons, inputs, chat, tables and labels. 500 for text, 700 for emphasis and buttons. The optical-size axis keeps small text open. JetBrains Mono appears only in these docs and the handoff, never in the product. {{ tnumTxt }}

Team names are made of player words and never get hyphenated. They're cut with an ellipsis, and the full name shows on hover, on focus and via aria-label.
RulesSet lang="de" on <html>. Body text and info-sheet copy use hyphenate. Headings use text-wrap: balance, plus &shy; where a compound is known (Tiebreaker-Pool, Teamwahl).
Never hyphenatePlayer names, team names, game names, buttons, badges or numbers. Buttons get shorter copy instead („Veto halten“, not „Veto einlegen und bestätigen“).
AccessibilityBody text never goes below 15 px on desktop or 16 px on mobile (16 px also stops iOS from zooming into inputs). Nothing is smaller than 12 px. Text reflows at 200 % zoom without horizontal scrolling. Never put text in all-caps Bungee.

Hard offset shadows

{{ s.k }} {{ s.use }}

Textures

Paper, lightDot grid, 22 px, 10 % ink. App background only.
Stage fog, darkWarm spotlight from above, vignette below. Animated smoke sits on top (Smoke page).
StickerDie-cut rim, white in both modes, soft drop. For confetti and gutter decor. Never on cards or chat.
Team patterns16 px SVG tiles, 26–34 % ink. Utility pattern-team-3.
Elevation logicHard shadows mean “you can touch this”. On hover an element lifts 1 px (the shadow grows); on press it sinks into its shadow (translate 4/4, shadow 0). Flat surfaces with no shadow can't be interacted with.
Don'tNo blurred shadows except soft (tooltips, toasts). No gradients on surfaces. Use only one orange stage shadow per element in dark mode, never orange on orange.
AccessibilityOutlines are ≥ 3:1 against the background (ink 17:1, cream 16:1), so component edges pass WCAG 1.4.11. Under forced-colors the shadows drop out and the borders stay.

Choreography rules
  1. Overshoot is physical. ease-pop and ease-spring only for things that land: avatars, stickers, the coin, emotes, a climbing rank. Buttons, tabs, toasts, sheets and modals use ease-out or ease-snap and never bounce.
  2. One hero at a time. During a moment (spin, reveal, coin, podium) everything else holds still. Smoke slows to 50 %, idle loops pause.
  3. Container → content → decor. The panel lands first, then its rows stagger in, then stickers.
  4. Exits are faster than entries (× 0.7) and never bounce.
  5. Server time rules. The wheel, clip, countdown and reveals start from server timestamps. The client may only ease in and out around them.
  6. Stagger caps at 400 ms total. With more than 10 items, the stagger shrinks; it never gets longer.
  7. Never block input. Every animation can be interrupted, and a new state jumps straight to the end of the old one.
