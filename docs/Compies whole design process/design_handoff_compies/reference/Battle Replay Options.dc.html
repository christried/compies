9Battle replay

Der Abend als Schlacht.

The last 1.0 ticket. Replays a finished session: the parties fight, after every game you see who stands where, with a game cut-in and a random generic gaming line in between. Same data in all three: Freitags-Zocken, 6 games, 4 teams, Eulen lead after game 5 and Gurken win the last one. Hit „Abspielen“.

Meine Wahl: 9aThe only one where the standing after every game is readable at a glance, and it scales to 8 teams and to Jeder gegen jeden with 8 people. The lead-change stamp is borrowed from 9c for lead changes.
9aRace

Wettrennen

Every party is a token on a lane; points push it toward the finish. The game winner jumps, lead changes get a stamp, Compibert cheers along without words.

Start
≈ 1.45 s per game, 10 s total. Lanes = parties, up to 8.
Stark
  • Standing after each game is obvious
  • Scales to 8 lanes, also for solo mode
  • Lead changes read as overtaking
Schwach
  • More race than battle
  • Little drama when one party runs away
9bBrawl

Arena

Party shields in a ring. The game winner charges the middle, everyone gets knocked back, and shields grow with points. The ranking strip re-sorts below.

Start
≈ 2.7 s per game. Strip re-sorts with Flip.
Stark
  • Literally a battle, the most physical
  • Comic sound words fit the Memphis style
  • Growing shields show momentum
Schwach
  • Standing only readable in the strip
  • Crowded beyond 6 parties
9cArcade

Kampfbildschirm

Each game as a fighting-game round on the dark stage: game winner vs. runner-up, health bars, K.O. The ladder on the right keeps the totals.

Start
VS
≈ 3.4 s per game, 21 s total.
Stark
  • The most „gaming“; everyone gets the reference
  • Big moments: FIGHT!, K.O.!
  • Dark stage, sticker look
Schwach
  • Only two parties per round on screen
  • Longest; gets samey by game 6
Gilt für alle drei
  • Gaming lines are generic, never brand or game quotes: „GG!“, „Clutch!“, „Respawn in 3 …“, „Achievement freigeschaltet“, „Comeback-Modus“ … drawn without repeats from a pool of 16, extendable.
  • Controls: play/pause, skip to the next game, speed 1× / 2×. Starts paused in the archive; autoplays once after the ceremony if the host chooses „Replay ansehen“.
  • Reduced motion: per game a still frame (cut-in card + standing) with a 200 ms crossfade; „Weiter“ button instead of a timer.
  • Data: per-game placements from the result entry; no new fields needed.
