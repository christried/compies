05 Übergänge und Zustandstabelle

Template for the state machine in the app. The prototype above runs exactly these rules.
  1. Two layers. Basis = context, persistent (Ruhe, Umschauen, Schläft, Mitfiebern, Spannung, Tanzen, Nachdenken, Verwirrt). Reaktion = one-shot with a duration (Winken, Lachen, Wütend, Jubeln, Traurig). He shows the reaction if there is one, otherwise the base.
  2. Never hard-cut. Every switch tweens all parts to rest in 250 ms while the next state starts; overwrite: 'auto' hands each property over. Mouths crossfade in 100 ms.
  3. Exit point. If he is in the air (root y < −3), the switch waits for the landing, at most 320 ms.
  4. Priority. Verwirrt 4, Jubeln and Traurig 3, Lachen and Wütend 2, Winken 1. Equal or higher replaces the running reaction and restarts the timer; lower is dropped, never queued.
  5. Base changes during a reaction are silent. The reaction ends into the new base.
  6. „Traurig“ during „Jubeln“: same priority, latest wins. He lands, blends into Traurig, 4 s from now, then back to the base.
  7. Errors interrupt everything and block reactions until the connection is back; then he waves once. Same after „Schläft“ when someone joins.
  8. Room emotes: he reacts at most once per 4 s to other people's emotes; your own emote always triggers.
  9. Reduced motion: same machine. States become held poses with a 200 ms crossfade, loops are paused at their start, blinking stays.

06 Lebendigkeit

Runs under every state, independent of the state timeline, so nothing reads as an obvious loop.
BlinzelnRandom every 1.6–5.2 s. Lid to y 0 in 70 ms power2.in, back in 110 ms power2.out. 18 % double blink. Returns to the state's lid height. Off while sleeping.
Atmen und Schwanzbody scaleY 1.035, scaleX .99, 1.6–2.1 s picked per mount, sine yoyo. Tail ±4/7/11°, 1.5 s, offset 0.22 s per segment. States only change timeScale: .2 Spannung, .3 Schläft, .4 Traurig, up to 2.6 Mitfiebern.
Auge folgt dem Cursorpupil-left via quickTo .4 s power3, max .42 rx / .36 ry, full deflection at 220 px. neck rotation ±6° quickTo .7 s. Paused in Umschauen, Spannung, Nachdenken, Verwirrt, Schläft. Touch: follows the last tap.
ZufallIn Ruhe, every 2.2–5.5 s one micro action: 45 % head tilt (−8…6°), 27 % arm twitch, 28 % small hop. Loop lengths are randomised per instance, so two Compiberts on screen never sync.
