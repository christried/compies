Die großen Entscheidungen.

Entschieden: 3b 4a 5a 6b 7a. The final specs live in Compibert Rig.

Five decisions for Compibert (2b): boot, presence in the app, interaction, award ceremony and emotes. Every option runs live on the real rig. Hit „Abspielen“, or poke and drag where it says so. Each section ends with my pick, so you can just say „passt“ or override.

3Boot

Wie startet die App?

The first second people see. Plays on the first visit of a session; returning within 24 h skips straight to the content. Reduced motion: wordmark and Compibert fade in, 200 ms.

Meine Wahl: 3b + 3cSprint-in on the first visit, because it sells the sprinter body. The O-peek for every load after that, as the loading indicator.

These three are live: try them directly on the stage. Eye-follow runs in all of them.

5aTap toyGewählt

Anstupsen

Each tap triggers a random reaction: laugh, hop, wave, squash, shrug. Five taps within two seconds and he gets angry.

5bPhysical

Hochheben

Grab and drag him around. He dangles, panics, falls when you let go and lands with a squash. Drop him from high up and he is dizzy.

Zieh ihn hoch
Follow: quickTo 0.18 s. Swing = −1.4 × pointer velocity, max ±28°. Fall ∝ √height, squash 0.78.
Stark
  • Physical, very satisfying
  • Perfect for the empty lobby while waiting
  • Shows off the rig
Schwach
  • Clashes with drag on the game board
  • No room for it in the header or dock
5cHands off

Nur schauen

No click. His eye follows you and he waves when the pointer comes near, at most every four seconds.

Fahr über ihn
Hover = wave 1.9 s, cooldown 4 s. Not focusable, aria-hidden.
Stark
  • No conflict with any UI interaction
  • Never annoying
  • Cheapest to build
Schwach
  • Least fun
  • On touch, all you get is the eye following
