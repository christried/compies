# Phase 1 – Style Exploration

> **Attachment:** `00-product-brief.md`
> **Goal:** Explore several style directions on their own and in sensible combinations, so we can pick one direction for the design system.

---

## Prompt (copy)

We are building **COMPIES**, a web app for groups of friends who compete in challenges (video games, board games, anything). The attached product brief contains the full scope. Read it completely before you start. Important for the style:

- **Mascot "Compibert":** a small, cheeky dinosaur (a Compsognathus). Original character design, no references to films or brands.
- The app is a **live experience**. Everyone sits at their PC at the same time while one host runs the session. It features a lucky wheel, vetoes, a coin flip, a live leaderboard and a big award ceremony.
- **Lots of motion and effects** that feel satisfying and funny, but never get in the way of using the app.
- **Desktop first**; mobile must be fully functional.
- The **UI is German**, so long words like "Terminabstimmung" must work in layouts.
- Up to **8 parties** at once, so we need **8 clearly distinguishable team colors**.

### Task

Explore the following style archetypes **first each on its own**, then in **all sensible combinations**:

1. **Swiss Style**
2. **Memphis**
3. **Hand-Drawn Style**
4. **Pixel-Art Style**
5. **The style of this website:** https://www.siteinspire.com/website/13528-company-picnic
6. **Abstract the style of this website** and play around with it: https://bestwebsite.gallery/sites/sotd/2024/11/07/ko-fi

Analyze both websites yourself and name what defines their style (colors, typography, shapes, illustration, layout, motion) before applying it.

### Motion references

Consider these for every direction:

- **Boot animation when the app starts**, like a game console booting up, but in our style: https://recent.design/i/8sy6r7f-asmr-portfolio-interactions
- **Interactions and animations like these** are explicitly wanted. Follow them where they fit the style, or derive our own style from them: https://recent.design/i/gzkgota-david-heckhoff
- **Smoke in the background:** https://curated.design/sites/s/76446/
  - Turn this into a **background pattern**: moving smoke everywhere, in different shapes and colors.
  - Lots of motion, but **subtle and satisfying**, never distracting.

### Deliverables per style direction

For every **single direction** and every **combination** you consider sensible:

1. **Style tile:**
   - color palette (incl. 8 team colors)
   - typography (headline, body, numerals for leaderboards and countdowns)
   - shapes and edges
   - textures
   - icon style
2. **Compibert sketch** in this style: full body, plus a small head at avatar size
3. **Key screen, desktop:** the **lobby with game board**. Teams, bench and stands with avatars, plus the chat/activity panel on the right.
4. **Key moment, desktop:** the **lucky wheel** right after it stops:
   - the clip is playing
   - the 60-second veto countdown is running
   - the veto button is visible
5. **One mobile screen:** the lobby on a phone
6. **Motion sketch:** a short interactive prototype of the smoke background and one micro-moment (e.g. picking up an avatar and dragging it into a team)

### Combinations

- Propose **at least 4–6 combinations**. Examples:
  - a Swiss grid with Memphis accents
  - pixel art for game moments on top of a calm base system
  - hand-drawn for Compibert and emotes on a clean base
- Justify each combination in 2–3 sentences.
- Also name combinations that do **not** work, and why.
- For each one, check whether **light mode, dark mode or both** fit the style. Most people play on PC, often in the evening.

### Wrap-up

- A **side-by-side comparison** of all directions
- Your **recommendation** (top 3), rated by:
  - fun and character
  - readability during live use
  - distinguishability of 8 team colors
  - how well the direction carries lots of motion
  - mobile suitability

**Don't build a full design system yet.** That comes in phase 2, after I've picked a direction.
